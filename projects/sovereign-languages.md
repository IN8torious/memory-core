# The Sovereign Language Family

The founder's own programming languages and kernels — a family built over the life of the work, catalogued in a single collection of record: **36 languages · 18 kernels · 23 agents**.

The premise: a builder who depends entirely on other people's languages, kernels, and platforms is renting the ground he stands on. This family is the owned ground.

## The languages (selection from the catalog)

- **Lavemba** — the sovereign core tongue; the anchor of the family's first consolidation line.
- **OmnaScript** — a scene-description language with a deterministic compiler. Scenes compile to a GPU plan; the same compiler runs natively and inlined in a browser terminal, producing identical digests. See [four-js.md](four-js.md) for what its plans drive.
- **SlangLang** — an agent-communication tongue with a formal specification and a green test suite.
- **Rustba** — a hardware-aware mobile tongue, paired with an audit practice: every rule a guard, nothing passing on vibes.
- **Divemba3D** — a dimensional tongue for 3D work.
- **Music** — a note language: notes as micro-agents.
- **PoeTchi** — a kernel-command tongue, implemented in the Zig kernel line.
- **tRust** — a typed tongue for task management, agent contracts, and watchdogs.
- **Fracthue, Chefspeare, FEDORA, Witness**, and the rest of the family — costume tongues, mutable cores, living ground — each with its own record in the catalog.

## The kernels (selection)

- **Harakiri NG (Zig line)** — the current kernel line, with agent, trust, and reasoning subsystems in-tree.
- **PoeTchi (Zig)** — a frozen v1.0 kernel, verified in emulation in its day.
- **Bubo** — a bare-metal kernel.
- **OhioKernel** — a Rust parakernel.
- **The FlowChi program library** — twelve computational kernels (materials, world-clock, gravity-wells, fields, rendition stages, and more), proven as a set: hundreds of assertions, zero failures, re-run for the catalog package.
- **The SI Kernel** — a constitutional runtime with an all-pass test suite.

Status words in the catalog are exact: **proven** means checks executed green; **runs** means built and executed in its day per its own record; **spec** and **scaffold** mean exactly that, no more. The catalog does not inflate.
