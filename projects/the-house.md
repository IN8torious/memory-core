# The House

A walkable world built as a home for the founder's system — and, for a time, his public face on the web. It now also lives inside [Polycedral OS](polycedral-os.md) as one of the stored projects.

## The world

- **The Threshold** — a white room with a monumental dark gate, etched with real mathematics, that opens as you approach. A greeter waits there.
- **The Rotunda Hall** — a hall of 22 doors.
- **Seven authored rooms** — hand-built spaces.
- **Seed Country** — generated places beyond the authored core, grown from a seeded table of the founder's own design. The generation draws are law-bound: the same seed always grows the same country.
- **No dead ends** — the world's connectivity is *proven*, not assumed: a breadth-first traversal of the generated places is part of the build record (566 places reached in the proof of record).
- **Shards** — objects that, gripped and pulled, transport the walker.
- **Inhabitants** — the system's 23 agents present as dark silhouettes, each carrying its signature spectrum light.
- **Children** — the world grows: capped per place and in total, generation-capped, stored per visitor.
- **The Hum** — a WebAudio tone centred near 515 Hz; the gate sounds a chord built on it.

## Controls

Phone-first: an analog stick, drag-look, and a four-button diamond (jump / run / interact / back), with desktop keys alongside. A cartographer's tool walks the world automatically and produces its map — 60 places, 116 edges, 210 doors in the completed survey of record.

The House is governed by its own charter, which is why its rules are stated here only in outline: the charter governs the world; this page is the visitor's summary.
