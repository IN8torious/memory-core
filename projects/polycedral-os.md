# Polycedral OS

**Live at [n8toriousai.com](https://n8toriousai.com).** Repository: `IN8torious/Polycedral-os`.

A card-based desktop operating system that runs in the browser — every card a working window, no dead chrome. It grew out of the founder's original card-desktop design and was expanded into a full environment:

- **Working windows for real projects** — earlier builds (a walkable house, an agent-system simulation, a world of small citizens, a 4D composition) are stored *inside* the OS and run in their own tabs, byte-faithful to their originals.
- **A real terminal** — it runs the founder's actual OmnaScript compiler in the browser; the same source compiles to the same digest in the page as it does natively.
- **A factory** — grows seeded agents from molds drawn from the real language family.
- **A polyhedral room** — the system's rosters rendered as geometry: the agent residents as signature beads held in gravity wells, the kernels in named polyhedra, the languages in an outer band, with collision shells ("reverse boundaries") and combination events logged to a register.
- **The system's own tables of record** drive the Projects and Downloads views — 36 languages, 18 kernels, 23 agents, per the catalog of record.

Standing design law for the OS: every card a working window; the top search presence is the system's own cognition seat; nothing decorative that pretends to function.

The build history runs in numbered passes, each closed by a receipt — scroll behaviour, the polyhedral turn, deepening under founder steering, visit tabs, a shell-first loading architecture that retired a long-standing context-loss failure, the project store-in, and a floor pass with real soft shadows. The receipts are the history; the live site is the current state.
