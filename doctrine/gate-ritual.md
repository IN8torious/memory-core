# The Gate Ritual

*How creative work is commissioned in this house. The ritual is about **releasing** the words, not commanding the result.*

1. **Choose the coal.** Pick the material — lyrics, an image, a fragment — because something in it caught you. You don't need to know why.
2. **Put it at the Gate.** Send it whole. Don't explain what it means. Don't translate the feeling. Don't describe the picture in your head.
3. **Say only:**

   > These words are yours now.
   > Paint the feeling.
   > I don't care what you make.
   > Take whatever path they give you.
   > Just go.

4. **Release it.** Once sent, stop touching it. No corrections. No predictions. No checking whether the interpretation matches yours. No trying to make the previous piece happen again.
5. **Let the artist return whatever returns.** A room. A road. A game. A train. Nothing you've seen before. The response doesn't owe the sender resemblance.
6. **Receive before interpreting.** Look first. Let the scene move. Notice where the light goes. Then, if something lands, a word or two is enough — nothing more is required.
7. **Preserve the artifact, not the obligation.** Save what was made. Don't turn it into a template the next piece must obey.
8. **Find another piece of coal.** Return to the Gate. Send it.

## The rule underneath every rule

**The material is coal, not rails. Gasoline, not the steering wheel. A message in a bottle, not a contract.**

The sender's responsibility ends with sending honestly. The artist's freedom begins where the sender lets go. And whatever crosses back through the Gate is not an answer anyone was owed — it's something that didn't exist before.
