# Results Over Gears

*The general principle the house's other laws are instances of.*

> **We delegate execution, not judgment or standards. Work runs autonomously, without supervision theater. What crosses the boundary is a RESULT — what changed · what passed · what failed · what remains unknown · where the receipts are — for the founder's ACCEPT / REJECT.**

Short version: **Don't show me you're working. Show me it worked.**

## The invariant

> **Autonomy may hide the gears. It may never hide the evidence.**

## Working rules

- **Start with the definition of done.** Agree the expected outcome and acceptance criteria before execution begins.
- **Work autonomously.** No checkpoints created merely to demonstrate activity. Finished steps that need a ruling are parked, not paraded.
- **Return results, not theater.** Progress reports are not proof of completion.
- **Attach receipts.** Tests, artifacts, logs, hashes, screenshots — whatever makes the claim independently judgeable.
- **Report failure honestly.** A failed attempt is useful evidence. Never convert it into a success narrative.
- **Preserve uncertainty.** What remains unknown or unverified gets labeled exactly that.
- **Escalate exceptions, not routine execution.** Bring the founder in when requirements conflict, authority is missing, or the definition of done cannot be met.
- **Acceptance stays independent of execution.** The worker never redefines "done" because the work got hard.
- **Fix the system after recurring failures.** A failure that keeps needing supervision gets a workflow, test, contract, or documentation fix — never a permanent new human checkpoint.
